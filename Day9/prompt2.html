Nutrition sources & methodology

Energy & macro targets Calculated with the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR equation, scaled by a standard activity factor (1.2–1.9), then split 20% protein / 50% carbohydrate / 30% fat.
Micronutrient targets Adapted from adult Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) ranges published by ICMR-NIN (Indian) and NIH Office of Dietary Supplements (DRI) tables, simplified by age and gender.
Food composition values Typical per-100 g averages for common home-cooked preparations, compiled in the style of USDA FoodData Central and the Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT). Actual values vary with brand, cut, ripeness, and cooking method.
Risk analysis logic A rule-based pattern matcher — it compares your logged intake against target ranges and flags well-known nutrition-risk associations (e.g. low iron, low B12 on a vegetarian diet). It is not a clinical or diagnostic algorithm.

Educational disclaimer

NutriScope is built for learning, not treatment. It is a self-tracking and education tool intended to help you understand the general shape of your diet — it does not diagnose deficiencies, diseases, or conditions of any kind.

All calorie, macro, and micronutrient figures are estimates based on typical food averages and standard formulas. Individual needs vary with metabolism, health conditions, pregnancy, medication, and lab-confirmed deficiencies that a food log cannot capture.

Please consult a registered dietitian, nutritionist, or physician before making significant dietary changes, starting supplementation, or if the Risk Analysis section repeatedly flags a concern.
